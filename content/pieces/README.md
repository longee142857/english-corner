# English Corner pieces

Original reading passages, pair roleplay scripts, and debate motions for the English Corner app. There are no audio files and no copyrighted articles.

## Counts

| Area | Items | Files |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Reading | 48 | `content/reading/a1.json` through `content/reading/c1.json` |
| Listening | 36 | `content/listening/a1.json` through `content/listening/c1.json` |
| Debates | 46 | `content/debates/motions.json` |
| Total | 130 | `content/pieces/manifest.json` |

Levels are `A1`, `A2`, `B1`, `B2`, and `C1`. Reading passages for A2–C1 are 120–220 words. A1 passages are shorter. Each reading and listening item has at least three comprehension questions. `answer` is a 0-based index into `options`. Listening scripts are 8–16 turns and are meant to be performed by a pair. Each motion has arguments for both sides, useful phrases, and follow-up prompts.

`source` is `original` on every item.
